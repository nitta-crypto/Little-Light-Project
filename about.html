Our Story

A small beginning, guided by a big belief: every person deserves dignity, and every voice matters.

What we believe

Little Light Project is a youth-led human rights awareness initiative. It aims to help people learn about their rights, encourage empathy, and make space for voices that are too often overlooked.

We believe young people can start making a difference now—by learning, listening, sharing awareness, and choosing kindness.

DignityCourageCompassionHope

Our mission

To raise awareness about human rights and help make the voices of people in need louder and more widely heard.

Our work is growing, and our future plans will develop step by step.

Dear Friends, Supporters, and Members of Our Global Community,

I started Little Light Project with a simple but powerful belief: every person deserves to be seen, heard, valued, and treated with dignity.

I care deeply about our society. I realized that sometimes the voices of people in need are overlooked or unheard. I also realized that people do not always listen when the message comes from adults alone. That is why I believe in starting young.

I want to use my voice as a young person to help make the voices of the needy grow louder and be heard around the globe. I may be young, but I believe age should never stop anyone from standing up for what is right.

Little Light Project represents hope, compassion, courage, and the belief that even the smallest light can brighten someone's darkness. My dream is to raise awareness about human rights and inspire young people to understand that their voices matter, too.

Change does not happen overnight, but small acts of kindness, awareness, and courage can grow into something meaningful. I invite you to join me in building a world where no one's dignity is forgotten and no one's voice is ignored.

Because sometimes, all it takes is one little light to begin a brighter future.

With love, hope, and determination,

Nitta Wanjiku ♡

Founder & Director
